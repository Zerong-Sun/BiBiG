# The Broken Bridge

From Richard Holmes, *Footsteps* (1985); also cited in Hermione Lee's *Biography: A Very Short Introduction*.

## The Metaphor

Biography is a **broken bridge** into the past. You stand at your end and look across carefully, objectively, into the unattainable other side. You cannot walk all the way over. You bring the past back by other skills — crafts, research, and what Holmes calls "sensible magic."

## Footsteps as Vocation

Biography became for Holmes:

- Not only a book about someone's life
- A **tracking** of the physical trail through the past
- A **following of footsteps**

> You would never catch them; no, you would never quite catch them. But maybe, if you were lucky, you might write about the pursuit of that fleeting figure in such a way as to bring it alive in the present.

## Romantic Biographer

"Romantic" here means:

- **Involvement** with the subject's world — stamina, curiosity, empathy, adventure
- **Detachment** enough to see clearly — not uncritical adulation
- Willingness to travel, sleep rough, read on site, talk to locals
- Respect for landscape, weather, and the subject's actual routes (not tourist retracements)
