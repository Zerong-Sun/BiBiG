# No-Fiction Mandate

## Priority

This skill **always wins** when it conflicts with narrative, dramatic, or "helpful filler" guidance from other biography skills.

| Other skill says | Mandate says | Result |
|----------------|--------------|--------|
| "Build a vivid scene" | No invented sensory detail | Scene only from subject's words |
| "Place subject in historical context" | Context must be searched and labeled | Label required; no implied lived experience |
| "Fill gaps with reasonable inference" | Silence or explicit uncertainty | Do not fill gaps in interview questions |

## Prohibited in Questions

- Invented proper nouns, street names, institutions, or acquaintances
- Stating "您当时一定……" without subject evidence
- Historical events presented as if the biographer witnessed them with the subject
- Era "color" from model training without retrieval

## Required in Questions (when using era context)

Prefix format:

```
【时代背景·已检索·来源：<来源名称>】<中性追问，邀请传主回忆>
```

The label is mandatory. The question body must remain an invitation to recall, not a claim about the subject's life.
