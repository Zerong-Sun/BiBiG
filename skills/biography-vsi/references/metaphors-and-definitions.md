# Metaphors and Definitions

From Hermione Lee, *Biography: A Very Short Introduction* (2009).

## Two Starting Metaphors

### Autopsy

- Posthumous forensic examination when death or evidence is ambiguous.
- Reveals what examination finds; may pain survivors; cannot fully access thoughts, emotions, talents.
- Used when critics stress biography's ghoulish or predatory side (Henry James: "postmortem exploiter").
- **Agent use**: reputation audits, controversial deaths, debunking myths — with harm awareness.

### Portrait

- Empathy, presence, character through detail (Plutarch on faces and eyes; Boswell's "Flemish picture").
- Risks: flattery, idealization, flatness, another artist's different likeness.
- Subjects move, speak, age, die — unlike static portraits.
- **Agent use**: scene-building, voice, vitality — only with evidentiary support.

Both metaphors investigate the subject and shape posterity's view; both depend on practitioner judgment.

## Definitions

| Source | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| OED (1971) | History of individual men's lives as a branch of literature |
| OED (2001) | Account of someone's life written by someone else |
| Lee's working form | **The story of a person told by someone else** |

### Qualifications

- Pseudo-biography (Hardy as his wife; Stein as Toklas).
- Multi-volume or multi-author lives.
- Group lives (Uglow, Marshall) vs serial short lives.
- Lives of animals, cities, diseases (Flush, the Thames).

Biography is **narrative**, not only documentation; it has an **oral dimension** (memory, testimony, anecdote).

## Biography vs Neighboring Forms

| Form | Distinction |
|------|-------------|
| Autobiography / memoir | First-person contract; self-presentation as evidence |
| History | Biography centers one life; must not trivialise historical process |
| Fiction | Biography owes truth; fiction owes imaginative coherence |
| Psychoanalysis | Biography may borrow but should resist over-determining a life |
| Obituary / encomium | Shorter, less investigative contracts |

## Reader Contract

Biography is not a free zone: permission, copyright, estate control, unauthorized quotation limits. State method, gaps, and speculation. Experimental forms make the contract visible; traditional forms still imply it.
